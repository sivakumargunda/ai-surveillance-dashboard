## Tips for Maximum Engagement

### Do

- Post GIF + clear caption together
- Ask a question in comments
- Respond to all comments within 2 hours
- Tag relevant people/companies
- Use LinkedIn native video/GIF, not links
- Keep captions scannable with short lines
- Include numbers/metrics
- Tell a story

### Don't

- Post without a strong headline
- Use generic "like and share" CTAs
- Ignore comments
- Post the same thing multiple times
- Use spammy hashtags
- Make it too salesy
- Use link shorteners
